# Homepage surface brief

Scope: `/` (the homepage). `/projects` inherits this world in Operate mode.
Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: freelance clients first — often non-technical Philippine
tourism and hospitality owners — deciding whether to hire; engineering hiring
managers second.
Action: Start a project (to `#contact`); secondary: Résumé, View work.
Proof: three products of his own; 15 projects with live status; four payment
gateways, each attached to a project; one NFC card system; six on-site photos;
four real, attributed testimonials. Every claim derives from `lib/data.ts`.
Constraints: WCAG 2.2 AA; status never by colour alone; no motion runs longer
than five seconds; no count-ups, parallax or scroll-linked animation.
Unresolved: case-study pages are a later phase (board rows link to live sites
until then); OCS control-panel interior needs the client's clearance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Projects are running systems, not thumbnails — a live board with honest
status. Refuses the category default of a hero over a grid of screenshot cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm graphite ground, panels one step lighter, cream ink; sodium amber
is the only brand colour and green means live, nothing else. Hairline rules,
8px panels. Status by glyph shape — dot, ring, lock, square — plus a label.
Recursive: its sans voice for people, its MONO axis for systems.

STORY: The visitor learns he ships products of his own, WordPress platforms and
custom systems; believes it because every row is live and linked; starts a
project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky bar — `~/christian-raro`, nav, amber availability, theme
toggle. Left: eyebrow name and city, H1 "Full-stack developer." (40–64px), lede,
amber specialism line, stack chips, CTA row led by amber "Start a project".
Right: framed portrait. Below: four-cell mono proof band. Signature: live dots
pulse twice, then rest; board rows draw an amber left rule on hover.

FORM: Operator, B3 Signal — pinned by the user across three brainstorm rounds,
first of three presented worlds. Seed 2f5d7c31 run and acknowledged; the pin
beats the roll. RAISE (from the oscilloscope signal bench): everything measures
against one grid — panels, board columns and proof cells share one column
system, and every numeral is tabular.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
